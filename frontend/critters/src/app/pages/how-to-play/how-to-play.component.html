How to play

A small guide to the game for anyone who didn't grow up in the 90s.

A Tamagotchi is a virtual pet you can take care of. This one starts as a baby and evolves once — but how it evolves depends entirely on you.

Your pet can get sick, so you’ll need to give it medicine. It can become hungry, so be sure to feed it. Sometimes it just wants attention — and you must respond in time.

If it’s feeling low, play with it to raise its happiness. But be careful — playing too much will exhaust it!

What happens if you don’t take care of it in time? It dies.

And that’s the core principle of the game. Enjoy!
