About us

Two idiots and a dream

You might wonder why two people would be this stupid to recreate a 90s game for a Web Engineering module in uni, but you'll be surprised at what people come up with at like two am in the morning. This was a spontaneous idea born from the pitch that tamagotchi, or generally known as virtual pets, should be free but retain those older pixel-graphics rather than some of the modern versions that look uncannily smooth. Additionally, both devs had wishes of incorporating characters from their own favourite media and in the future expanding as they went.
